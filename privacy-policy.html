Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Nicholas Connors ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information in connection with the No-Show Recovery SMS notification service ("the Service"), provided to barbershop and salon businesses and their customers.

Information We Collect

When you book an appointment with a business using the Service, that business collects your name and mobile phone number as part of the normal booking process (for example, through Square Appointments). We use this information solely to send you appointment-related text messages on behalf of the business, such as no-show follow-up messages inviting you to rebook a missed appointment.

How We Use Your Information

Your mobile phone number is used only to send you SMS messages related to your appointments with the business you booked with. We do not use your phone number for marketing unrelated to your appointments, and we do not use it for any purpose other than the messaging described here.

No Sharing of Mobile Information

We do not share, sell, rent, or otherwise disclose your mobile phone number or any information collected in connection with SMS consent to any third party or affiliate for marketing or promotional purposes. Your mobile number is used exclusively to deliver the appointment-related messages described in this policy.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies depending on your appointment activity. You will typically receive a message only if you miss a scheduled appointment and are marked as a no-show by the business. Most customers will receive zero or very few messages per month.

Message and Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies. You can reply STOP at any time to opt out of receiving messages, or HELP for assistance.

Data Retention

We retain your phone number only for as long as necessary to provide the Service to the business you booked with, or until you opt out, whichever comes first.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to have your information removed, please contact us at nickconnors24@gmail.com.
